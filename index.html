Nothing about that save carries a date, a price, or a way to act on it, so it sits in a folder with forty others. Sharing it to Trip Stash instead gives the intent somewhere to live: Myra reads the video, caption and comments, identifies the place and the stays, and attaches a real price range from MakeMyTrip inventory.
